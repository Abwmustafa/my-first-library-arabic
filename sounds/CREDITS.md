# Sounds: where they come from

The web version plays these sounds when a child taps an animal, a vehicle, an instrument or a toy. Each clip is a few seconds cut from the recording named here, faded at the ends and set to an even volume.
Some animals make almost no sound, or no free recording of them was found, so they play the sound of what they do (crunch, munch, nibble, buzz, flutter, hop, running) or of a close relative: the houbara plays the little bustard.
The clips are shared under the same licence as the recording they come from.
